# Resume-Ready Project Descriptions

## One-line version

Built a Power BI dashboard monitoring inventory levels, delivery performance, and demand forecast accuracy across suppliers and warehouses.

## 2–3 line version

Designed and built a Supply Chain Efficiency & Inventory Optimization dashboard in Power BI, covering 150+ SKU-level records across products, suppliers, and warehouses. Used Power Query for data cleaning and DAX for KPIs including On-Time Delivery %, Forecast Accuracy %, and Inventory Turnover Ratio. Delivered category-, supplier-, and warehouse-level breakdowns to identify stockout risk and delivery bottlenecks.

## Strong ATS-friendly professional version

Developed an end-to-end Supply Chain Efficiency & Inventory Optimization dashboard using Power BI, Power Query, and DAX to track inventory levels, reorder status, supplier delivery performance, and demand forecast accuracy across product categories, warehouses, and regions. Cleaned and transformed raw supply chain data (handling nulls, duplicates, and inconsistent formatting) into an analysis-ready model. Built 10+ DAX measures — including Stockout Count, On-Time Delivery %, Forecast Accuracy %, and Inventory Turnover Ratio — and a multi-visual dashboard (KPI cards, supplier performance chart, forecast-vs-actual comparison, matrix table) enabling stakeholders to reduce stockouts and improve inventory efficiency.
