# Project Overview

## What this project is

A Power BI dashboard that tracks inventory health, delivery reliability, and forecast accuracy at the SKU level — sliced by product category, supplier, warehouse, and region.

## Why supply chain efficiency and inventory optimization matter

Inventory sitting in a warehouse is cash that isn't working, and a stockout is a lost sale (or worse, a lost customer) — both failure modes cost real money:

- **Stockouts and excess stock are two sides of the same coin.** A company can be over-invested in slow-moving categories while simultaneously running out of its bestsellers — you only see this by tracking turnover and reorder status together, not revenue alone.
- **Delivery performance is a leading indicator of stockout risk.** A supplier who's "usually" on time but drifting later each quarter will eventually cause a stockout before anyone notices in the aggregate numbers.
- **Forecast Accuracy % is the health check on planning itself.** If actual demand consistently overshoots or undershoots forecast, every downstream reorder decision inherits that error.
- **Category and warehouse breakdowns guide where to fix the process.** Knowing which warehouse or category has the worst turnover changes where operations should focus first.

## How companies use dashboards like this one

Supply chain and operations teams typically review a dashboard like this weekly: checking which SKUs need reordering now, which suppliers are trending toward missed deliveries, and whether the current month's actual demand is diverging from forecast. It becomes the shared reference in supplier review meetings, S&OP (sales and operations planning) cycles, and inventory strategy discussions.

## Job roles that use this kind of tool

- **Supply Chain Analyst** — builds and maintains the inventory and delivery reporting layer
- **Operations Manager** — uses warehouse- and region-level breakdowns to manage day-to-day fulfillment
- **Inventory Analyst / Planner** — owns reorder policy and stockout prevention
- **Procurement Analyst** — uses supplier delivery performance to inform sourcing decisions
- **Business Analyst** — supports the broader operations team with reporting infrastructure

## How MBA and business-analytics students benefit

This project builds fluency in the exact vocabulary supply chain and operations interviews test: inventory turnover, reorder point, on-time delivery %, forecast accuracy — not just a generic "operations dashboard." It also covers the full BI workflow end to end: cleaning messy supply chain data, modeling it, writing inventory-specific DAX, and telling a supplier/category performance story with visuals — directly relevant to manufacturing, retail, and e-commerce roles.
